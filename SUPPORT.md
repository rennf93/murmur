# Support

- Bugs and questions: open an [issue](https://github.com/rennf93/murmur/issues/new/choose). Include the murmur version (`npm ls -g murmur-it`), the CLI and its version, your OS, and `murmur status` output if it is relevant.
- Vulnerabilities: do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Documentation: <https://rennf93.github.io/murmur/>, including [Troubleshooting](https://rennf93.github.io/murmur/troubleshooting/).
